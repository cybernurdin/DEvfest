About SafeCity & Principles

SafeCity is an independent community safety awareness platform designed for the residents of Bamenda and the Mezam Division in North-West Cameroon.

⚠️ Formal Non-Affiliation Statement

SafeCity is independently built and operated. It is NOT an official emergency agency of the Republic of Cameroon, nor is it affiliated with the Ministry of Territorial Administration, municipal police, or Alberta Emergency Alert. The platform exists solely to disseminate verified civic notices and factual safety precautions. It does not replace national emergency dispatch services.

1. Core Safety & Moderation Principles

🛡️

No Precise Witness Exposure

We never publish exact GPS pins, private street addresses, or names of witnesses. Incidents are generalized to coarse neighborhood zones (e.g. Up Station, Commercial Ave).

🔍

Corroboration Before Critical Blast

Community reports are clearly marked as unverified until duty moderators corroborate facts through independent eyewitnesses or official utility updates.

🚫

Zero Rumor Amplification

Unverified gunfire, military movements, and sensitive allegations are blocked from automated broadcast to prevent rumor panics and protect civilian lives.

📝

Immutable Audit Trail

Every edit, retraction, and review decision is logged with an immutable revision number and timestamp to guarantee complete transparency.

2. Data Protection & Cameroon Law No. 2024/017

SafeCity adheres strictly to Cameroon Law No. 2024/017 of 23 December 2024 on the protection of personal data:

  • Anonymous Incident Ingestion: Citizens can submit safety hazards without creating an account or providing their real identity.
  • EXIF & Metadata Stripping: Uploaded evidence images are immediately purged of camera EXIF, GPS coordinates, and device identifiers on the client and edge.
  • Data Minimization: Only factual hazard descriptions are recorded. We proactively reject submissions that contain identifiable facial photographs or vehicle registration plates.
  • No Commercial Exploitation: SafeCity never sells user data, movement records, or location streams to commercial third parties or advertisers.
